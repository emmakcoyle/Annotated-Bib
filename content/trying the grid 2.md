Part of this project is the extensive interplay between text designed to be spoken and [footnotes]. Some of those footnotes will be visible on the screen during the video. The process of relating to the footnotes in the written document on which this video is based emulates both the way historical [pageant] forms exist as nesting dolls within contemporary versions and the flow of water, which can subvert a sense of [linearity]. In the footnotes, I [drift] from the Medieval history of the pageant and that time period’s conceptions of the body, to the close etymological ties between pageantry and marginality, to [mosquitoes] and [*John Donne]’s “[The Flea]”*, to the role of the [subject] within environmental aesthetics.
