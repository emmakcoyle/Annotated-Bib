*Poem · 24hr Neon Mag · 2019*

**

If any poem has 14 lines it’s a sonnet 

So this is my love letter 

Because sonnets are always love letters 

Even if it’s a bit sticky 

Since I’m not quite sure who it’s for 

But I can leave that part blank 

Just for now 

And instead say that I yearn 

For the way your lips soften 

even as they pull tight into a smile 

And the warmth of your shoulder blades 

As we lay in the dark 

What sweetness it is 

To finally be alone with you

**
