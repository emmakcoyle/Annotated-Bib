<div class="note-body" style="font-size:17px; max-width:640px; margin:0 auto;">

<h2 class="note-h2" style="font-family:'Fraunces', serif; font-weight:500; font-size:20px; margin:2.2rem 0 0.9rem;"><span style="color:var(--rubric);">¶</span> About me</h2>

<p><span class="dropcap">I</span>'m a New York-based writer, artist, and performer. My work moves between scholarly research, performance-making, curatorial practice, and arts administration — this archive is where those threads get to talk to each other instead of staying in separate folders.</p>

<p>I'm completing an MA in Performance Studies at NYU Tisch, graduating June 2026, and hold BAs in Dance and English Literature from the University of Maryland and in Cultural Performance Studies from the CUNY Baccalaureate program at Hunter College. I completed the Node Center for Curatorial Studies' International Curatorial Program in 2024–2025.</p>

<p>I co-founded Invited Dress Productions, a Brooklyn theater collective, and 24hr Neon, a DIY venue and literary magazine. I volunteer at the Center for Book Arts. My creative interests run toward durational performance, immersive theater, folk festivals, anti-hierarchical community arts structures, oral literary performance, magical realism, and Oulipian constraint-based practice — most of which shows up somewhere in this archive's organizing logic, whether or not it's named outright.</p>

<p>Recent and current work includes the novel <em>Incurable States of Being</em>, the stream-of-consciousness prose piece <em>Internal Wetlands</em>, and the artist's book <em>Manifesto for a Lover</em>, exhibited at the Fanzineist Vienna International Art Book Exhibition in 2025.</p>

<h2 class="note-h2" style="font-family:'Fraunces', serif; font-weight:500; font-size:20px; margin:2.2rem 0 0.9rem;"><span style="color:var(--rubric);">¶</span> How it's organized</h2>

<p>Every entry sits somewhere on a small grid — one axis for the kind of engagement (practice, theory, archive), one for the medium (text, image, performance) — plus a note on whether it's a summary, a critique, a creative reflection, or a tangent. The coordinate isn't decoration; it's a second way to browse, alongside the plain list.</p>

<h2 class="note-h2" style="font-family:'Fraunces', serif; font-weight:500; font-size:20px; margin:2.2rem 0 0.9rem;"><span style="color:var(--rubric);">¶</span> What lives here</h2>

<p>Source notes hold citations and annotations. Idea notes hold claims and concepts, kept separate from their sources so they can link to anything that touches them. Publication notes track my own finished work and what it drew on. Talks and lectures sit alongside interviews and readings — a source is a source, whatever form it came in.</p>
