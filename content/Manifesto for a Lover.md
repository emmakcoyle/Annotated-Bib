*Zine · Fanzineist Vienna International Art Book & Zine Exhibition · Semperdepot, Vienna, Austria · 2025*

> In looking for destiny, we are looking for a story. One with a beginning and an end. The end being now or where we dream ourselves to be in the future. We are looking for a resolution to our circumstances.

A short researched publication in the format of a zine, *Manifesto for a Lover* (2024), is an attempt to reconcile time, inter-relational communication, and memory organizational methods, while making a proposal for how to relate the self to the other without relying on Aristotelian conceptions of narrative plot. Eleven pages of contemplation with an additional ten pages of footnotes and citations allow room for both a continuous argument and more intimate digressions or expansions on the topic. Lyric prose and scholarly footnotes operate in non-hierarchical dialogue where the main text carrying the emotional and phenomenological argument and the footnotes examine the realm of the theoretical , engaging systems theory, environmental aesthetics, Aboriginal philosophies of time, and Buddhist conceptions of suffering. 

> To all things there is a beginning and an end.
> These are arbitrary as we cannot comprehend eternity. So we decide on moments that we consider decisive. Moments people meet, moments people die. We consider them endings and beginnings. But before the universe there was still the universe. God simply turned on the lights and we call it the first day.
