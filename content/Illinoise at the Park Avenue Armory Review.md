> While we can look at “Illinoise” at the Park Avenue Armory as a play or a dance concert, first and foremost it is a staged visual album. When considering where it fits into performance and critique, it most draws comparisons to Beyoncé's “Lemonade” or even the Talking Heads “Stop Making Sense” before it could be compared to a more “traditional” musical theater adaptation of a musician's discography. “Illinoise” might tell a story but it is most certainly more interested in sharing an emotional state and directing how the audience's mind explores themes like yearning and catharsis in Sufjan's album “Illinois,” on which the performance is based.
>
> From the first movements of entering the theater, it is noticeable how the space is set up in such a way that it resembles a rock concert.
